Murals

Mural & Environmental Design

Mural design is where I got to think about design on the largest scale possible. Working in collaboration with clients across hospitality, higher education, and healthcare, I designed large scale murals tailored to each client's brand guidelines, aesthetic vision, and the specific specifications of their space. The best part of every project was the puzzle of it: designing a cohesive mural for an entire room while working around real world obstacles like outlets, TVs, doors, and windows. Every installation required a design that felt intentional and seamless no matter what was in the way. Hotels, universities, and hospitals each came with their own unique challenges and brand standards, and learning to design within those constraints made me a sharper, more adaptable designer.
